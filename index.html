About Us

A New Standard of Quality & Trust in Saudi Arabia

Taqwa Trading and Services LLC Company is a Saudi-registered limited liability company headquartered in Al Aqeeq District, Riyadh. Founded with a clear vision to build a diversified business spanning Food & Beverage, Hospitality & Hotels, and Travel & Tourism.

Our phased growth strategy ensures sustainable expansion across multiple cities while maintaining the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction aligned with Saudi Vision 2030.
